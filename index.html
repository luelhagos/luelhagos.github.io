About

I build machine learning systems that ship, and I do research on speech and language technology for languages that most models ignore. Over four years at Ishango.ai I deployed production LLM systems for clients in Germany, Canada and the UK: a RAG search agent on AWS, a recommendation system, a document QA assistant over 2 TB of content, and the data pipelines underneath them. I'm now a master's student in Data Science at Duke University.

My research is on text-to-speech and speech recognition for low-resource languages, LLM post-training and instruction tuning, and multilingual evaluation. I have published at IEEE ASRU and IJCNLP-AACL on multilingual evaluation and low-resource speech and language technology, and I have trained and deployed TTS and ASR models for low-resource languages.
